Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Loupe is a free AI chat and image-generation tool. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • If you don't sign in: nothing is stored beyond your current browser session. Your chat history exists only in your browser's memory and disappears when you refresh or close the tab.
  • If you create an account: we store your email address (or basic profile info if you sign in with Google/Microsoft), your saved chat history, and your app preferences (like theme and custom instructions), so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to enforce a daily fair-use message limit. It is not permanently logged or linked to your identity.

How your messages are processed

To generate a response, your message is sent to the following services as part of answering your question:

Google Gemini API — processes your chat messages and any files you attach, to generate a response.

Tavily — used only for questions that appear to need current information (like news or scores); your question is sent to Tavily to retrieve relevant web results.

Pollinations.ai — receives your text prompt when you use the "Imagine" image generation feature.

These providers process the request to return a result; Loupe does not control their internal retention practices. Consult their own privacy policies for details on how they individually handle data: Google Gemini API Terms, Tavily Privacy Policy.

Where account data is stored

If you create an account, your data (email, saved chats, preferences) is stored using Google Firebase, a cloud database service. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond making Loupe work for you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use your conversations to train any AI model ourselves.

Your choices

  • You can use Loupe fully without ever creating an account.
  • If you have an account, you can clear your chat history or delete your account entirely at any time from Settings → Account.

Children

Loupe is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.
